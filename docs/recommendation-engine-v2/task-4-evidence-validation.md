## Eligibility

- **LOWER** requires a positive current seller price, at least two distinct fresh competitor prices with match scores of 0.65 or higher, and the existing price-gap threshold. The median is calculated from eligible observations. The exact candidate must be below the current price, at or below the eligible median, and approved by MarginGuard with applicable fees and a valid safe floor. Eligibility and freshness are checked before persistence.
- **RAISE** remains unavailable. A future implementation needs reliable positive price-response evidence, valid price and fee inputs, and MarginGuard approval of the exact raised price. Competitor stock, including unknown stock, provides no evidence of the seller's ability to raise prices.
- **REORDER** requires a fresh inventory snapshot, known supplier lead time, a positive observed or qualified recorded-sales rate, and a positive reorder proposal from the existing dedicated engine. It can proceed with insufficient competitor evidence because it uses seller inventory and sales.
- **HOLD** covers insufficient evidence and unsafe proposals. A blocked price remains visible in guard audit evidence only. It is cleared from the approved price fields and action. No eligible competitor data produces null market statistics.

## Confidence

Confidence is a rule-based label for the available evidence. It does not express a statistical probability or predictive uncertainty.

| Label | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| LOW | HOLD, a downgraded action, or an eligible action with signal below 0.50. |
| MEDIUM | Eligible action with signal at least 0.50 and below the HIGH requirements. |
| HIGH | Eligible action with signal at least 0.75 and additional support. For LOWER, this requires at least three comparables scored at least 0.88, at least 80% eligible price coverage, known stock for eligible comparables, and no run-level quality flags. For REORDER, this requires observed demand with complete coverage. |

`recommendation_context.evidence_assessment` stores the raw action signal, completeness counts, per-field freshness summary, matching and reorder evidence reliability, null predictive uncertainty, and the resulting action and label. The provenance record stores the snapshot score. Reason codes include `STALE_COMPETITOR_DATA`, `INSUFFICIENT_SALES_HISTORY`, `MISSING_FEE_POLICY`, `UNKNOWN_MARKET_AVAILABILITY`, `MODEL_UNAVAILABLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_ACTION_EVIDENCE`, and `MARGIN_GUARD_BLOCKED` where applicable. `MODEL_UNAVAILABLE` refers to a predictive model, since this path computes deterministic signals.

The Bangla explanation is generated from the final decision. It cannot choose the action, confidence, or approved price. The save boundary rejects invalid monetary fields and non-finite numeric output. No forecast, incremental revenue, or profit claim is generated by the validator.
