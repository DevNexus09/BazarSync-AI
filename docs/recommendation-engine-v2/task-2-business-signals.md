# Task 2 · Inventory and pricing signals

## Rollout

`RECOMMENDATION_BUSINESS_SIGNALS_ENABLED` defaults to `false`. Set it consistently on the API and workers after deploying Task 1's snapshot migration and collecting fresh observations. Task 2 automatically uses Task 1's strict evidence path, including the pinned scrape run and market evidence preflight. Disabling Task 2 restores the previous analysis path. Task 2 adds no schema migrations or dependencies.

## Sales and inventory

- The service reuses the sales-velocity aggregation over closed seller business days. The default window is 30 days through yesterday. Completed and fulfilled sales count, test orders and invalid quantities are excluded, and returns and restocked refunds reduce net units. Net units are bounded at zero per sale.
- `OBSERVED` requires a complete product-specific coverage record spanning the full window and a product old enough to cover it. A covered zero remains zero sales. Uncovered positive demand qualifies as `ESTIMATED_FROM_RECORDED_SALES` only when the velocity service marks the history sufficient, sales span at least three days, and confidence meets the existing reorder threshold. Other cases are `INSUFFICIENT`. Recorded net units can still be reported for sparse histories.
- Stock cover divides current available units by a defensible positive rate. Zero demand has no finite cover estimate. Missing demand or inventory leaves cover unavailable. A known zero available stock with positive demand gives zero days of cover. The latest inventory snapshot must be no more than `RECOMMENDATION_INVENTORY_MAX_AGE_HOURS` old (48 hours by default), and its on-hand count must match the product's current count. Stock cover excludes incoming stock. The generic flow has no confirmed incoming signal to pass to the reorder engine.
- The existing reorder engine decides whether to recommend a purchase when the rate, inventory snapshot and actual supplier lead time support it. The generic flow does not use the specialized engine's default lead time or save a separate reorder plan. A missing supplier lead time leaves risk unavailable and blocks reorder. Competitor availability is scored only over known fresh stock observations, and never adjusts seller sales velocity.
- The API keeps its existing numeric coverage and risk keys. For Task 2, `stock_coverage_available` and `stock_risk_available` specify whether those values are meaningful. A numeric zero with availability false is a compatibility placeholder. The recommendation page displays unavailable risk explicitly. The existing nullable competitor stock count remains null when all classifications are unknown.

## Pricing and fallbacks

The legacy engine assumes 15% in fees. Task 2 uses Decimal values from Task 1 price snapshots, the existing fee resolver, PricingCalculator and MarginGuard. Fee precedence is product, category, then seller for the selected marketplace and effective period. Currency follows the BDT product-price convention and effective pricing policy. Unsupported fee or policy currencies block pricing. The exact proposed lowering price is the fresh market median rounded down to the configured increment. MarginGuard checks cost, fees, margin, price bounds and change limits, then rechecks that exact candidate before persistence. A failed final check saves HOLD and no suggested price. The general flow does not generate RAISE candidates. It prioritizes the dedicated reorder result and conservatively holds price if demand, inventory or lead time is unavailable.

Offer evidence distinguishes gross revenue per unit at the listed price, gross margin per unit after product cost, and contribution margin per unit and rate after known variable fees. The pricing foundation models fixed per-order fees as one unit per order for this calculation. Missing cost or fee evidence leaves the affected measures unavailable. Contribution margin excludes overhead and does not establish net profit. Estimated profit and incremental revenue are unavailable. The existing numeric revenue-opportunity field remains zero. Task 2 stores evidence and reason codes in recommendation context and uses a deterministic Bangla explanation of the final action.
